# Gallery

The preceding pages work through `automesh`'s commands one feature at a time —
segmentation, defeaturing, dualization, remeshing, smoothing — each on a small,
purpose-built case.

The Gallery instead collects **common mesh models**, drawn
from the computational geometry and computer graphics literature as well as
from engineering practice.

Models are split by where they come from:

- [Academic Models](academic.md) — standard test surfaces from the
  computational- eometry / computer graphics literature, chosen for specific
  geometric challenges (sharp features, high genus, curvature variation, thin
  structures).
- [Industry Models](industry.md) — parts representative of real
  mechanical design and analysis work, chosen for practical relevance
  (manufactured features, assemblies, engineering benchmarks) rather than
  geometric pathology.
